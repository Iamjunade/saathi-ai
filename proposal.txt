1. The Problem
When people face a real-world problem, the hardest part is often not getting an answer—it is knowing what to do
next. A person may not know which hospital, government department, emergency service, route, form, or support
channel is relevant. Existing chatbots mainly provide information; they do not reliably convert human intent into a
verified action.
SAATHI addresses this gap by creating a single human interface to complex societal infrastructure.
2. Our Solution
SAATHI is not another AI chatbot. It is a multimodal, agentic response platform through which users can speak
naturally, show an image, or provide text. SAATHI understands the situation, assesses risk, selects the right
specialist capability, asks for authorization when needed, coordinates the appropriate service, executes the next
action, and follows up until the case is resolved.

 THE PROPOSED SYSTEM
 Four high-impact workflows powered by one agentic platform
01 MEDICAL COORDINATION
Voice → risk assessment → nearby hospital discovery → user authorization → simulated contact → case tracking →
follow-up.
Boundary: SAATHI supports triage and coordination; it does not diagnose disease.
02 DISASTER RESPONSE
Voice/location → local risk estimation → affected-zone assessment → safe route/shelter discovery → personalized
response.
Prototype risk model can use rainfall, temperature, elevation, drainage, historical incidents, roads and population.
03 CIVIC ASSISTANCE
Voice → transcription/language detection → intent & entity extraction → department classification → complaint
draft → structured case → tracking.
Turns an everyday complaint into an actionable, traceable case.
04 ACCESSIBILITY & VISION
Camera → OCR/vision → scene or document understanding → simplified explanation → spoken response.
Designed to reduce information barriers through multimodal and multilingual interaction.
5. Technology & Architecture
Experience: React Native + Expo mobile app, Next.js + TypeScript web dashboard. Backend: NestJS API with
Python/FastAPI AI/ML services. AI: reasoning model + structured tool workflows, specialist agents and
deterministic safety policies. Voice: real-time speech-to-speech with WebRTC. Data: PostgreSQL + pgvector,
Redis; event-driven workflows. ML: PyTorch, scikit-learn/XGBoost, evaluation and safety benchmarks.

6. Trust, Safety & Human Control
• Sensitive actions require explicit authorization; every tool declares risk, permissions, data shared and audit
 requirements.
• Emergency handling uses a governed safety policy rather than allowing an LLM to make unrestricted critical decisions.
 
• Structured AI outputs are validated server-side; critical actions are never triggered by free-form model text.
 
• Privacy follows data minimization, purpose limitation, consent, retention controls and auditability.
 
• The system never claims an action succeeded unless a verified tool result confirms it; simulated integrations are
 clearly labeled.

8. Innovation & Impact
• One interface, many domains: medical, disaster, civic and accessibility use cases are unified by the same response
 loop.
• From conversation to outcome: SAATHI treats every real-world issue as a Case with status, actions, authorization and
 resolution state.
• Trust as infrastructure: consent, safety policies, audit trails and verifiable tool outcomes are part of the core
 product—not add-ons.
• Designed for inclusion: voice-first interaction and multilingual multimodal support reduce dependence on technical
 literacy.
• Scalable foundation: future agents can extend the platform to education, employment, public services, financial safety
 and family assistance.
